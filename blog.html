Kannur, located on the northern coast of Kerala, is one of the most beautiful and culturally rich destinations in South India. Known for its peaceful beaches, historic forts, traditional art forms, and lush greenery, Kannur offers a perfect mix of nature, heritage, and relaxation. If you are searching for the best places to visit in Kannur, this guide will help you explore the beauty and charm of this coastal city.

Why Kannur is Famous?

Kannur is often called the cultural capital of North Kerala. The district is known for its traditional art forms like Theyyam, handloom industries, historical monuments, and scenic landscapes. The calm environment and natural beauty make Kannur one of the top travel destinations in Kerala.

Beaches: The Most Beautiful Places to Visit in Kannur

Beaches are among the top places to visit in Kannur because of their clean surroundings and relaxing atmosphere.

1. Payyambalam Beach

Payyambalam Beach is one of the most visited beaches in the city. The beach is famous for its golden sand, sunset views, and peaceful vibe. It is one of the best places to visit in Kannur for evening walks, photography, and coastal breeze.

2. Muzhappilangad Drive-In Beach

Muzhappilangad Driving Beach is one of the longest driving beaches in Asia (extending over 4 kilometers). Visitors can drive vehicles directly along the firm beach sands, enjoy coastal water sports, and catch panoramic sea views. This destination is always included in lists of top places to visit in Kannur.

3. Meenkunnu Beach

Meenkunnu Beach is perfect for travellers who love quiet and less crowded locations. The natural beauty and calm atmosphere make it one of the hidden gems to visit in Kannur for relaxation away from the crowds.

Historical Places to Visit in Kannur

Kannur has a rich historical background influenced by the Portuguese, the Dutch, and local kingdoms. Many travellers explore these historical places to understand the region’s heritage.

St. Angelo Fort

St. Angelo Fort is one of the oldest and most famous forts in Kerala. Built by the Portuguese in 1505 and later fortified by the Dutch and British, the fort offers commanding views of the Arabian Sea and the natural harbor of Mopila Bay. It remains one of the top-rated historical places to visit in Kannur.

Kannur Lighthouse

Standing adjacent to Payyambalam Beach, the Kannur Lighthouse offers visitors a 360-degree panoramic vantage point overlooking the Arabian Sea, rocky promontories, and swaying coconut groves.

Hill Stations and Nature Destinations

Nature lovers can explore several green, misty, and scenic spots nestled in the Western Ghat ranges and plateaus of Kannur.

Madayipara Hill Station

Madayipara is a bio-diverse laterite hill plateau renowned for rare wildflowers, ancient shrines, and as a revered backdrop for sacred Theyyam performances and seasonal birdwatching.

Palakkayam Thattu

Palakkayam Thattu is a breathtaking hill station surrounded by misty mountains and lush forests. Trekking, camping, and photography are popular activities here. Sitting at an altitude of approximately 3,500 feet, it is one of the coolest places to visit in Kannur during weekends.

Paithalmala

Paithalmala is another scenic hill station known for trekking trails and panoramic valley views. During monsoon and winter, the hills become blanketed with clouds, creating an enchanting, magical atmosphere for nature enthusiasts.

Cultural Attractions & Theyyam

Kannur is deeply connected to Kerala’s rich culture and spiritual traditions. Visitors can experience sacred ritual performances, vibrant festivals, and local artistry.

Theyyam Rituals

Theyyam is one of the most celebrated ritual dance forms in Kerala, recognized internationally for its striking face makeup, towering headgear, and profound spiritual energy. Watching a live Theyyam performance in local kavus (sacred groves) is an unforgettable experience.

Food Experience in Kannur

Kannur is globally famed for authentic Malabar culinary treasures. From fresh coastal seafood, fragrant Thalassery biriyani, and soft pathiris to evening Malabar tea snacks, exploring local food stalls and dining spots is an essential part of visiting Kannur.

Shopping and Local Markets in Kannur

Apart from beaches and tourist attractions, shopping is also an enjoyable experience in Kannur. The city is famous for handloom products, traditional fabrics, spices, and handmade crafts. Visitors can explore local markets and textile shops to experience the authentic craftsmanship of North Kerala.

Kannur handloom products are popular across India because of their superior quality and traditional weaving techniques. Many travellers purchase bedsheets, cotton fabrics, and traditional Kerala clothing as souvenirs.

Best Time to Visit Kannur

The best time to explore the places to visit in Kannur is from October to March. During this season, the weather is pleasant with clear skies, mild temperatures, and optimal conditions for sightseeing, beach strolls, and hill station treks.
